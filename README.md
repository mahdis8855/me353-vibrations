# ME 353 Engineering Vibrations — interactive demos

**Free response of a damped system** — https://mahdis8855.github.io/me353-vibrations/

Drag the damping ratio ζ (or m, k, c), the natural frequency ωₙ, and the initial conditions x₀, v₀, and watch the mass–spring–damper, the response x(t) with its envelope, and the roots in the complex plane change together. Single HTML file, no dependencies; works offline.
